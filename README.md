> Note: This is a public case-study repository. It documents the project goals, feature scope, design direction and technical approach. The production codebase remains private.

---

## Overview

**BUBECK’s Brot & mehr** is a traditional family bakery in Stuttgart-Luginsland with a long local history. The goal was to create a modern, responsive website that presents the bakery’s craft, product range, story, opening hours and contact information in a professional way.

The project goes beyond a simple brochure website. It includes a content management direction with an admin panel, allowing the business to manage products, categories, offers, hero slides, contact inquiries and site settings without technical knowledge.

**Live website:** [https://baeckerei-bubeck.de](https://baeckerei-bubeck.de)

---

## Project Goals

- Create a modern online presence for a traditional local bakery
- Present more than 90 years of bakery tradition in a fresh and emotional way
- Build a product catalog for breads, baked goods and specialties
- Allow the business owner to update products, offers and content independently
- Improve local visibility for Stuttgart-Luginsland and nearby search queries
- Provide clear opening hours, location information and contact options
- Build a mobile-first experience for everyday customers
- Add privacy, SEO and accessibility foundations suitable for a German business website

---

## Challenge

The bakery needed a digital presence that matched its reputation, history and product quality. The previous online presence did not fully communicate the value of the business and did not provide a flexible way to update daily content.

Main challenges:

- The old online presence did not reflect the bakery’s quality and tradition
- Products, daily offers and opening hours needed to be editable without developer support
- The bakery needed stronger local search visibility around Stuttgart-Luginsland
- The website had to feel warm, trustworthy and modern without losing the traditional character
- Visitors needed quick access to products, opening hours, location and contact options

---

## Solution

The solution was a modern bakery website with a custom CMS/admin panel, local SEO structure and a warm visual direction that combines tradition with modern usability.

The website presents the bakery through a structured homepage, product catalog, history section, contact area, Google Maps/location information, reviews and legal pages. The CMS/admin direction allows products, categories, offers, hero slides, contact inquiries and site settings to be managed directly by the operator.

Key solution points:

- Modern responsive website for a traditional bakery
- Dynamic product catalog with categories and product presentation
- Custom CMS/admin panel for content management
- Hero slider and storytelling sections
- Opening hours logic with open/closed status direction
- Local SEO with Schema.org and structured content
- Contact form with reCAPTCHA v3 direction
- Cookie consent and German legal page structure
